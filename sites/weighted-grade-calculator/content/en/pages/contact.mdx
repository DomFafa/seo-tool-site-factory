## Contact

For questions, corrections, privacy requests, or feedback about the weighted grade calculator, email:

```text
contact@weighted-gradecalculator.com
```

## Helpful details to include

When reporting a calculation issue, include the category weights, scores, and the result you expected. Do not send private student records or unnecessary personal information.

## Response scope

WeightedGrade can help clarify how the calculator works, but it cannot verify official school grades, gradebook access, instructor policies, or transcript records.
