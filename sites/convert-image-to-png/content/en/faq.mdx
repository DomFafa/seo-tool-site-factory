## Frequently asked questions

### Does this tool upload my image?

No. The v1 converter uses browser APIs to create the PNG output locally.

### What image types are supported?

The initial version supports JPG, PNG, and WebP files.

### Why use PNG?

PNG is useful when you need a lossless image format or when transparency matters.
