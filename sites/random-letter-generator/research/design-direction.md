# Design Direction

Reference: Sejda homepage style provided by the user.

## Direction

Build a quiet utility page with Sejda-inspired restraint:

- White top navigation.
- Green primary action color.
- Coffee/brown supporting text and warm beige surfaces.
- Centered hero, concise copy, and immediate tool access.
- Soft tan wave band behind the tool, inspired by the user's screenshot.
- Tiny mint dot accents in the corners, used sparingly.

## Palette

- Primary green: `#25c28a`
- Deep green: `#14986b`
- Coffee text: `#3a312b`
- Muted coffee: `#8a6a4f`
- Warm background: `#fbf7f1`
- Wave fill: `#efe2d3`
- Pale wave: `#f8f1e9`
- Surface: `#ffffff`

## Layout

Avoid marketing-page excess. This is a working utility site. The tool should be visible immediately after the hero. Cards use 8px radius to stay closer to Sejda's practical UI.

## Differentiator

The memorable visual is the calm wave shelf that carries the generator, paired with crisp letter tiles and copy controls.
