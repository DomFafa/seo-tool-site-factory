## How to use the generator

Choose the alphabet source first. A-Z is best for general use, vowels and consonants are useful for practice activities, and custom letters are best when a game or worksheet has its own allowed set.

Set the count to the number of letters you need. Use no-repeat mode when you want a shuffled set. Turn it off when repeated letters are acceptable.

After generating, choose the copy format that fits your task. Lines work well for worksheets. Commas work well for spreadsheets. Groups are useful for teams and classroom rounds.
