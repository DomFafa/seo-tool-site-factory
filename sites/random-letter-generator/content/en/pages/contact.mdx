## Contact

Before a production domain and support inbox are selected, use the project contact path from the repository owner to report issues or request corrections.

## Helpful details

When reporting a tool issue, include the alphabet mode, count, no-repeat setting, copy format, group size, and the browser you used. Do not send private, sensitive, or regulated data.

## Support scope

We can review tool behavior, content accuracy, accessibility issues, and layout problems. We cannot verify legal drawings, official contests, or regulated random selections.
