## FAQ

### Can I generate more than one random letter?

Yes. Set the count to generate one letter or a list of letters.

### Can I generate letters without repeats?

Yes. Keep no-repeat mode enabled when each letter should appear only once. If the requested count is larger than the available letters, the tool shows a clear error.

### Can I use only vowels or consonants?

Yes. Choose the vowel or consonant preset, or enter your own custom letters.

### Can I use custom letters?

Yes. Enter letters separated by spaces, commas, or nothing at all. Duplicate letters are removed from the custom alphabet before generation.

### Which copy formats are supported?

You can copy letters as grouped rows, one letter per line, comma-separated values, or space-separated values.

### Are the generated letters truly random?

The tool uses browser pseudo-randomness. It is suitable for games, classroom activities, prompts, and sample data, but not for cryptographic security or regulated drawings.

### Are my custom letters saved?

No. The generator runs in your browser, and raw custom input is not sent to analytics.
