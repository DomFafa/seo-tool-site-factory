## About this tool

Random Letter Generator helps you create one letter or a list of letters from A-Z, vowels, consonants, or a custom alphabet. It is built for quick classroom activities, games, alphabet practice, prompts, team labels, worksheets, and sample data.

## What it is best for

Use it when you need letters that are easy to copy into worksheets, games, classroom rounds, spreadsheets, or simple mock data. You can choose whether letters repeat, switch case, group the output, and copy the result in a clean format.

## What it does not replace

This tool is not designed for cryptographic randomness, legal drawings, official contests, password generation, or audited random selection. It uses browser pseudo-randomness for practical utility tasks.

## Privacy-first design

Letter generation runs in your browser. Custom alphabets and generated letters are not sent to analytics as raw user input.
