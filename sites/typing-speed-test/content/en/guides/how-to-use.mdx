## How to use this typing speed test

1. Choose a duration that matches your goal.
2. Read the prompt before you begin.
3. Type naturally and focus on accuracy.
4. Review your WPM, accuracy, and error count.
5. Repeat the same test length to compare progress.

## Tips for better results

Keep your hands relaxed, sit comfortably, and avoid looking at the keyboard. If your error count is high, slow down for a few attempts and rebuild rhythm before chasing a higher WPM score.
