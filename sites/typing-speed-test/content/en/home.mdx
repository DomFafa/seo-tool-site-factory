## Test your typing speed online

Use this typing speed test to measure how quickly and accurately you type. Choose a test length, type the prompt, and review your words per minute, characters per minute, accuracy, and errors.

The test is designed to stay focused on one task: help you understand your current typing speed without distractions. It works well for quick practice sessions, keyboard warmups, and checking whether your accuracy is improving over time.

## What your result means

- **WPM** estimates words per minute using the common five-character word convention.
- **CPM** shows correct characters per minute.
- **Accuracy** compares correct characters with the text you typed.
- **Errors** count typed characters that did not match the prompt.

For the best result, try the same duration several times and compare your average rather than judging one attempt.

## Privacy note

The typing test runs in your browser. The full text you type is not sent to analytics or to a server by this tool.
