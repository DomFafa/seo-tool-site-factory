## Frequently asked questions

### What is WPM?

WPM means words per minute. Many typing tests estimate one word as five characters, so the result is based on correct characters typed during the test.

### Is accuracy more important than speed?

Accuracy should come first. A high WPM score is less useful if many characters are wrong. Build accuracy first, then gradually increase speed.

### What test length should I choose?

A 30-second test is useful for a quick check. A 1-minute test gives a more stable result. Longer tests are better for measuring typing endurance.

### Does this tool upload what I type?

No. This tool calculates the result in your browser and avoids sending the full typed text to analytics or to a server.
