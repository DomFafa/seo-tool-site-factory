## Frequently asked questions

### What contrast ratio do I need for WCAG AA?

WCAG AA needs a contrast ratio of at least `4.5:1` for normal text and at least `3:1` for large text. UI components and graphical objects commonly use the `3:1` threshold.

### What contrast ratio do I need for WCAG AAA?

WCAG AAA needs at least `7:1` for normal text and at least `4.5:1` for large text. AAA is stricter and may not be practical for every brand palette, but it is useful for critical reading experiences.

### What counts as large text?

Large text is commonly interpreted as at least 18pt regular text or 14pt bold text. Small labels, helper text, form errors, and navigation links should usually be checked as normal text.

### Does this prove ADA compliance?

No. This tool checks color contrast thresholds only. ADA compliance and accessibility conformance depend on many more factors, including keyboard access, labels, semantics, focus states, alt text, and the full user flow.

### Can I use this for buttons and icons?

Yes. Use the UI component verdict for important button boundaries, icon shapes, focus indicators, and graphical objects. Text inside the button should also pass the relevant text threshold.

### Are my colors uploaded or stored?

No. The checker runs in your browser. Raw foreground and background color values are not sent to analytics.
