## Contact

For corrections, accessibility notes, privacy questions, or feedback about the color contrast checker, email:

```text
contact@colorcontrastchecker.online
```

## Helpful details to include

If you are reporting a calculation issue, include the foreground color, background color, expected ratio, and browser. Do not send private project data unless it is needed for the report.

## Response scope

Contrast Check can clarify how the checker works, but it cannot certify legal compliance or audit a full website from a single color pair.
