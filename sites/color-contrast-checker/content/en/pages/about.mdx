## About this checker

Contrast Check is a focused color contrast checker for designers, developers, content editors, and product teams who need a quick WCAG contrast grade before a color pair ships.

The tool calculates relative luminance and contrast ratio from foreground and background colors, then marks the common AA and AAA thresholds for normal text, large text, and UI components.

## What it is best for

- Checking text colors against page or card backgrounds.
- Reviewing button labels, form labels, helper text, and error states.
- Copying a short contrast note into a design review or issue.
- Finding a nearby foreground color when a pair fails.

## What it does not replace

This tool does not replace a full accessibility audit, legal review, screen reader test, keyboard test, or design-system review. Color contrast is only one part of accessible interface design.

## Privacy-first design

The checker runs in your browser. Raw color values are not sent to analytics.
