## Frequently asked questions

### What is a contrast ratio?

A contrast ratio measures the difference in relative luminance between two colors. The scale runs from `1:1` for identical colors to `21:1` for black against white.

### What contrast ratio do I need for normal text?

WCAG AA normal text needs at least `4.5:1`. WCAG AAA normal text needs at least `7:1`.

### What contrast ratio do I need for large text?

WCAG AA large text needs at least `3:1`. WCAG AAA large text needs at least `4.5:1`.

### Why does my color fail even though it looks readable to me?

Perception changes with screen quality, font size, font weight, lighting, vision differences, and surrounding colors. WCAG thresholds provide a measurable baseline rather than relying only on visual judgment.

### Does passing this checker make a page accessible?

No. Passing contrast is important, but accessibility also depends on keyboard access, labels, semantics, focus states, error handling, alt text, and more.

### Are my color values saved?

No. The calculation runs locally in your browser and raw color values are not collected in analytics events.
