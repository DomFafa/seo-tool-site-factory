## How to create cursed text

1. Type the text you want to distort.
2. Adjust the intensity slider.
3. Copy the cursed text result.
4. Paste it into your message, username, post, or design.

## Keep it readable

Cursed text can become difficult to read quickly. Use a lower intensity for usernames, short messages, and social bios. Use higher intensity only when the visual effect matters more than readability.
