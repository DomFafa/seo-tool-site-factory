## Frequently asked questions

### What is cursed text?

Cursed text is distorted text created by adding marks around letters. It is often used for creepy, glitch, horror, meme, or gaming effects.

### What is Zalgo text?

Zalgo text is a common name for text that uses many Unicode combining marks above, below, and through letters to create a chaotic appearance.

### Why does the text look different in some apps?

Apps and devices render Unicode marks differently. Some platforms may limit, normalize, or display the marks in a less dramatic way.

### Can I clean cursed text?

Yes. The generator includes a cleaned output option that removes combining marks from the input.
