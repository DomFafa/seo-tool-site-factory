## Frequently asked questions

### What is cursed text?

Cursed text is text with Unicode combining marks added around letters. The marks make normal words look distorted, creepy, broken, or glitchy.

### Is cursed text the same as Zalgo text?

Usually, yes. Zalgo text is a popular name for the heavy version of cursed text, where marks stack above, through, and below letters.

### Does cursed text work on Discord, TikTok, Instagram, and Reddit?

It often works, but each platform renders Unicode marks differently. Use light or medium intensity for names and bios, and reserve heavy cursed text for short messages.

### Why does cursed text look different on different devices?

Fonts, operating systems, browsers, and apps all handle combining marks differently. The same copied text can look taller, tighter, or less intense depending on where it appears.

### Can cursed text break apps or websites?

Normal short cursed text should not break modern apps, but very heavy output can make lines tall, hard to select, or uncomfortable to read. Reduce intensity if a platform behaves badly.

### Can I remove cursed text and get normal text back?

Yes. Use clean mode to remove Unicode combining marks from cursed or Zalgo text and recover the readable base characters.

### Is my text private?

Yes. This tool generates and cleans cursed text in your browser. Raw input and generated output are not sent to a server by the tool.
