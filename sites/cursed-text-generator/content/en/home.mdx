## Generate cursed and glitchy text

Type a word or sentence, adjust the intensity, and copy a cursed text effect. The generator adds Unicode combining marks around letters to create a messy, distorted, Zalgo-style look.

Use lower intensity for readable creepy text and higher intensity for stronger glitch effects. If the output becomes too hard to read, reduce the intensity and try again.

## Privacy note

The effect is generated in your browser. The text you enter is not sent to a server by this tool.
