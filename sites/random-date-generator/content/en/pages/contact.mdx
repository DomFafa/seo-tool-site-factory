## Contact

Before a production domain and support inbox are selected, use the project contact path from the repository owner to report issues or request corrections.

## Helpful details

When reporting a tool issue, include the date range, count, weekday filter, unique-date setting, output format, and the browser you used. Do not send private or sensitive data.

## Support scope

We can review tool behavior, content accuracy, accessibility issues, and layout problems. We cannot verify official schedules, legal deadlines, contest results, or regulated random drawings.
