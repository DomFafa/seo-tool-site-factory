## About this tool

Random Date Generator helps you create one date or a list of dates from a selected range. It is built for quick test data, planning examples, classroom prompts, content calendars, and everyday date-picking tasks.

## What it is best for

Use it when you need dates that are easy to copy into spreadsheets, forms, mockups, or sample datasets. You can filter by weekday, choose whether results must be unique, and switch between common output formats.

## What it does not replace

This tool is not designed for cryptographic randomness, legal drawings, official scheduling systems, or applications that require audited random selection. It uses browser pseudo-randomness for practical utility tasks.

## Privacy-first design

Date generation runs in your browser. Selected ranges and generated dates are not sent to analytics as raw user input.
