## Frequently asked questions

### What is an anagram?

An anagram is a word or phrase made by rearranging letters. For example, “listen” can be rearranged into “silent”.

### Does the solver have every English word?

No word list is perfect. The solver uses a built-in English word list and should be treated as a practical helper, not an official dictionary for every game or competition.

### Can I search for shorter words too?

Yes. Lower the minimum length to include shorter words that can be made from your letters.

### Does the tool send my letters to a server?

No. The search runs in your browser and avoids sending the raw letters to analytics or to a server.
