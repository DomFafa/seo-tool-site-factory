## Frequently asked questions

### What is WPM?

WPM means words per minute. A common typing-test convention treats five characters as one word.

### Is accuracy more important than speed?

Accuracy matters first. Fast typing with many mistakes is less useful than steady typing with fewer corrections.

### Does this tool upload what I type?

No. The v1 tool calculates results in your browser and avoids sending the full typed text to analytics.
