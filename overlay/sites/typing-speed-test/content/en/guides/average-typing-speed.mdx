## What is a good typing speed?

A good typing speed depends on the task. For casual writing, consistency and accuracy often matter more than a high WPM score. For transcription, data entry, or timed work, speed becomes more important.

Use the typing test on this site to track your own baseline and focus on gradual improvement.
