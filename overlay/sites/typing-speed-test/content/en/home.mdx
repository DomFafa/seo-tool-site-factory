## Test your typing speed online

Use this typing speed test to measure how many words per minute you can type while keeping track of accuracy and mistakes. The test runs in your browser and is designed to stay focused on the core task: type the prompt, finish the timer, and review your result.

## What the results mean

- **WPM** estimates words per minute using the standard five-character word convention.
- **CPM** measures correct characters per minute.
- **Accuracy** compares correct characters with the text you typed.
- **Errors** show how many typed characters did not match the prompt.

## Privacy note

This v1 typing test runs locally in the browser. It does not send the full typed text to analytics or to a server.
