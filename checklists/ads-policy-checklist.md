# Ads Policy Checklist

- [ ] Site lifecycle is `monetizable` or `launched`.
- [ ] Content Gate passed.
- [ ] Ads do not appear near copy/download/start/convert buttons.
- [ ] Ads are not disguised as tool controls.
- [ ] Ads do not block the tool on mobile.
- [ ] Ads are not under misleading labels.
- [ ] Draft/noindex/under-construction pages do not load ad scripts.
- [ ] Ad slots reserve layout space.
- [ ] Result/download areas remain visually separate from ads.
